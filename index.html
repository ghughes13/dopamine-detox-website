Privacy Policy

Dopamine Detox · Effective November 6, 2026

Dopamine Detox does not collect, send or share any data. Your settings and stats stay in your browser. We never receive them.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Dopamine Detox browser extension, published by Cybershift Labs ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code, and it makes no network requests of its own.

What is stored on your device

The extension needs a small amount of information to work. It is kept in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), which the extension does not sync or upload.

Blocked visits are stored as a number per day. The extension does not record which pages you tried to visit.

It also keeps a temporary note of whether a lock is active and which sites it covers (chrome.storage.session). Your browser clears this when it closes.

How website addresses are used

The extension only acts on the built-in sites and any custom sites you add. When one of them is locked, it redirects the page to the extension's lock screen before the site loads, and does the same for any tabs already open on that site.

The original address is passed to the lock screen so it can show the site's name and offer a link back when the lock ends. That address stays on your device, and the extension does not save it.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, transfer or share user data, because we do not have any. No third party receives data from the extension.

Your choices

You can change your sites and schedules in the extension's options at any time, although some changes are unavailable during a lock. Uninstalling the extension removes all of the data it stored from your browser.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side.

Children

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your browser and other sites

This policy covers the extension only. Your browser, its extension store and the websites you visit have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
